What does "bilingual" mean here — which languages?
+
All subjects are taught in both Hindi and English. Concepts are explained in Hindi for clarity, then reinforced in English — so students are comfortable with both languages during exams.
Which classes and streams do you offer coaching for?
+
We offer coaching for Class IX, X (Madhyamik), and Class XI–XII across all three HS streams — Science, Commerce, and Arts. Subjects include Hindi, English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and more.
Can I join online if I'm not from Rishra?
+
Absolutely. Our online batches are open to students from anywhere in India. Classes are conducted live via Google Meet or Zoom. You'll get the same quality of teaching, study materials, and mock tests as offline students.
What are the available batch timings?
+
We offer morning (6–9 AM), afternoon (12–3 PM), evening (5–8 PM), and weekend-only batches for both online and offline modes. Contact us at 9038537320 to know the current schedule and availability.
Are recorded lectures available if I miss a class?
+
Yes. All online sessions are recorded and shared with enrolled students. You can watch them at your own pace — making it easy to catch up if you miss a live class.
How do I get my doubts cleared?
+
Doubts can be cleared during the live class itself, in dedicated weekly doubt sessions, or via our WhatsApp support group where faculty responds quickly between classes.
How do I enroll — online or offline?
+
Simply fill the enrollment form at the bottom of this page, or call DKS Sir directly on 9038537320. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours with all the details.
